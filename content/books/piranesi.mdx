A strange, twisting, and deeply original tale. The book is often cited as one of the best of the century so far, and I really liked Susanna Clarke's first novel, [Jonathan Strange and Mr Norrell](/books/jonathan-strange-mr-norrell), so I had high expectations.

It is hard to describe even with spoilers, and I agree with many other reviewers that going in knowing little is best. It is a mystery that is revealed over time to great effect, but until the turning point it really is a bit weird. It's worth sticking with, though. You can tell there is a high level of literary craft here (as with Jonathan Strange and Mr Norrell), but I did not find it as engaging. Solid, but not on my best-of lists.
